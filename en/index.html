In 2022 I was heading into a new role at a tech company when the call came. Impacted by layoffs. The move I’d planned was gone before it started. What followed was a year of rebuilding: not just a career, but a clearer sense of what I actually wanted next, and how to make the market see it.

That’s the work I do now. I coach senior executives through career transitions: the strategic side (positioning, narrative, target companies, interview preparation) and the human side (the loneliness, the self-doubt, the loss of identity that nobody talks about in a job search).

Clarity Session

A 60-minute 1:1 session for senior leaders weighing a step-up, a step-out, or a return to the job market after a transition.

In 60 minutes, we work together to:

  • pinpoint where you are today and what’s no longer aligned with your next move
  • identify 1-2 concrete levers you can act on in the coming weeks
  • clarify whether a structured coaching path could support you, and how

This isn’t an informational call, it’s a working session, with you at the center. Come with a real question or crossroads, and leave with a clear action plan, regardless of what happens next between us.

Designed for managers, executives, and senior leaders at a decision point: stepping up, stepping out, or re-entering the workforce.

Questions

What is the Clarity Session?

A 60-minute working session, not an informational call. You bring a real question or crossroads and leave with a clear action plan, whatever happens next between us. Together we also clarify whether a structured coaching path could support you.

How do the sessions work?

Online on Google Meet, 60 minutes each. In person is possible on request. After every session you receive a written report.

How much does a program cost?

It depends on the program. We define it together after the Clarity Session. If you decide to continue with a coaching program within 30 days, the €300 of the Clarity Session is fully credited toward it.

Is what we discuss confidential?

Yes. I work under the ICF code of ethics, which includes confidentiality.
